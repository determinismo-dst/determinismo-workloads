## 8. Library gaps filled (Jank/R3/Search/Common.lean; sdk/lean untouched)

- `exhaustUnder`: `exhaust` restricted to the timelines under a decision prefix, so a world is searched in partitions that each end `complete` or `found` (sdk's `nextPath` backtracks into the prefix).
- `censusUnder`: every timeline of a partition, violations counted per class (property name) with the first example and up to 12 more for `unexplained`; this is what makes "complete, nothing new" a statement about a whole table rather than a first finding.
- `estimate`: Knuth's estimator of a world's timeline count from uniform paths (to size worlds before choosing bounds; it underestimates heavy-tailed worlds, so exact counts are given where a formula exists).
- `exploreMixed` (SplitMix64-mixed seeds; consecutive `mkStdGen` seeds draw first decisions in a cycle of the option count) and `tallySeeds3` (a census over random programs).
- `Search.lean`'s loops over `List.range maxRuns` allocate the whole list: the round 3 walks use `[0:n]` ranges (a 60 M bound costs no memory).
- Program grammar bounded by a budget of compound forms (complete enumeration by size rather than depth) with exact counting for round 2's grammar (`r2Count`).
