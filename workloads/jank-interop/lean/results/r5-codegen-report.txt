2a. Proved (no sorry, no native_decide, no new axiom; `#print axioms` in r5-codegen-axioms.txt
shows only propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound)
- The generator as a decision tree. genE/genFnCall are `partial`, so nothing can be proved about
  them. Tree.lean defines `DT` (a decision node per recorded decision, one child per option; a
  decision with one option is no node, as Decisions.next records none) as a monad, with
  `leaves` (timelines), `walk` (a path's value and decisions) and the counting lemmas
  (`leaves_bind_const`, `leaves_dec`, `leaves_pick`, Sub.lean `leaves_pick_bind`,
  `bind_assoc`). Gen.lean's `genT` restates the clean branches of ProgSearch.lean 60–181 in it,
  with the same decisions in the same order, names and option counts. It is checked against the
  original (section 7): every depth-1 timeline, every timeline of the small depth-2 partitions,
  random depth-2 timelines; forms and decision lists identical.
- ★ `leaves_genT` (every depth d, every context without a recur target): genT d c t has exactly
  `cnt d (length c.locals)` timelines, `cnt` a closed recurrence over the 20 productions and 3
  fn kinds. The proof carries `recurArity = none` into every subexpression, so it also shows the
  clean grammar never builds a recur target (kind 14 always draws a `try`).
- ★ `world1`: the depth-1 world has exactly 3,464 timelines (round 2's complete walk: equal).
- ★ `world2`: the depth-2 world has exactly 354,197,993,668 timelines. Round 4's Knuth estimate
  (≈3.3·10¹¹) was 7% low.
- ★ `leaves_genK1`, `subWorld_size`: the sub-world of 2d has exactly 9,246,393 timelines.

2b. The reduction used: distinct programs
`ProgSearch.body` (ProgSearch.lean 222–231) draws the form e, then compares Spec.run [e] with
jankRun [e] cfg, so the verdict is a function of e: timelines that draw the same program have
the same verdict. In the clean grammar a program is drawn twice only at a leaf (kind 2 with no
local in scope is the form 0, which kind 0 draws too; a pick among locals that repeat a name);
every other production writes what it drew into the form. `distinctCount` (Distinct.lean)
counts by that rule: 2,793 programs at depth 1 and 181,450,370,757 at depth 2. The count is
computed, not proved: it equals the number of distinct program texts on every depth-1 timeline
and on every small depth-2 partition (section 7).

2c. Not done: the property for every input, or a reduction that completes the world
Where it stops: the two root productions with three depth-1 subexpressions,
  let2 (kind 8): 312,527,871,808 timelines, 159,591,517,260 distinct programs;
  if (kind 10): 41,565,561,344 timelines, 21,787,771,257 distinct programs;
together 99.97% of the world. Every other root kind has at most two subexpressions and at most
28,263,447 timelines.

Why the property was not proved for every input. jankRun runs about 100 `partial` definitions
(Analyzer/Processor 30, Codegen/Processor 19, CppAst 14, Exec 11, Cpp 8, Surface 9, and others),
which are opaque to proofs. A total restatement would have to carry, besides the analyzer and the
code generator, a print/parse round trip of the emitted C++ text (Cpp.lean → CppAst.parseProgram)
and the interpreter of that text: a correctness proof of a compiler for this fragment.

Why no compositional reduction (the outcome of `(if c t e)` or `(let [n1 v1 n2 v2] b)` from the
outcomes of its subexpressions). jank's pipeline threads state across siblings by construction,
so "a subexpression means the same whatever its siblings are" is the statement that would need
the proof. Each of these points is a mechanism an earlier round found a deviation through:
  1. Lifted constants: one map per module keyed by very_equal_to_with_meta (ir/builder.cpp
     99–123, emitted at codegen/cpp_processor.cpp 2071 and 2160–2175; Ir.lean bLiteral): a
     literal in one sibling is the object another sibling gets (R42).
  2. local_to_loop_shadow: set at loop entry (ir/processor.cpp 270, 541), read by recur (422,
     439) and never restored (Processor.lean genLet): a sibling's loop leaves entries that a
     later recur reads by munged name (R39).
  3. A compound form's type and its children's positions depend on every branch:
     find_branch_common_type (analyze/processor.cpp 2741 for if, 1509 for case) and
     propagate_position (ir/processor.cpp 663, 756, 813) (R23, S57).
  4. The locals map is keyed by munged name (ir/processor.cpp 206, 249, 519; Processor.lean
     localsIndex/setLocalName) (R38).
  5. __rt_ctx->unique_string is one process-wide counter (ir/builder.cpp 107, 281, 307;
     cpp_processor.cpp 83, 1974; analyze/processor.cpp 1853, 2416): every identifier a sibling
     emits depends on what the siblings before it emitted.
The clean grammar avoids each mechanism's trigger, which is why it is a control; "it avoids the
trigger, so the siblings don't interact" is a statement about the code generator's state on every
program, the same correctness proof.
- Symmetry: renaming x↔y and :a↔:b would at most quarter the distinct programs (to ≈4.5·10¹⁰).
  Neither renaming can be proved to leave jankRun's outcome unchanged (C++ identifiers; keyword
  hashes in the constant map's key), and a quarter is not enough.
- Independence: no subexpression is dead for jank. A branch Clojure never evaluates is still
  analyzed, compiled and checked, and could fail to compile.
- Reduced estimate after what holds: 181,450,370,757 distinct programs, 181,379,288,517 of them
  under let2 and if. At the rate the sub-world ran (about 3,700 programs/s in eval mode and
  2,750/s in AOT on one core, with children smaller than the full world's), the distinct programs
  alone would take at least 570 core-days in eval and 760 in AOT; a walk over timelines, which is
  what a decision walk enumerates, at least 1,100 and 1,490.

2d. What was run instead (section 7): every root kind with at most one depth-1 subexpression
completely in both modes; the sub-world below completely in both modes; the two-subexpression
root kinds as far as the shared job slots allowed.
Sub-world (Sub.lean): root let2 or if, each depth-1 subexpression from the productions with at
most one leaf subexpression (kinds 0–5, 9, 13, 15–19). It meets every pair and triple of such
siblings: shared constants, loops and defs side by side, every leaf kind in the condition and
both branches, the let2 bindings over each other. Exactly 9,246,393 timelines (★ subWorld_size).
