  147-198). Invalid UTF-8 reaches the server's stdout exactly when byte 20 cuts a character (walk). No
  Clojure counterpart, no test.
- re-matcher with re-groups: the same matcher loop as re-seq in both implementations (core.jank re-seq ->
  re-find on one matcher; R81's new-string search), counted under .seq.
- R60's fix (11).
