How a BigDecimal reaches jank's code (read, not assumed):
- A BigDecimal is boost `number<cpp_dec_float<100>, et_off>` (include/cpp/jank/type.hpp 35): 16 limbs of 8 digits,
  a value with no scale. Literals: read/lex.cpp → big_decimal(string) → rd_string (round 3's replica).
- `+ - * /` (2 args), `quot`, `rem`, `inc`, `dec`, `abs`, `min`, `max`, `hash` inline (core.jank 1371-1435, 1616-1701,
  2476-2486); a BigDecimal is always an object, so math.hpp's object branches run; a native long or double literal
  meeting it takes boost's mixed operators, the value its boxed form gives (math.hpp primitive templates compute
  `l OP typed_r->data`): jank's answer does not depend on the path for a BigDecimal form (NumA7-2).
- Printing: big_decimal.cpp 157-175 → string_builder.cpp 240-249: `str()`, then ".0" when the text has no '.'.
- Clojure: BigDecimalOps (Numbers.java 964-1092) with *math-context* nil: exact add/multiply, divide exact or
  "Non-terminating decimal expansion; no exact representable decimal result.", quotient = divideToIntegralValue,
  remainder = remainder; minus(x, y) = add(x, ops(y).negate(y)) (162-165); toBigDecimal (298-322) converts a Ratio by
  dividing (so a non-terminating ratio throws first); hasheqFrom (1132-1145) strips trailing zeros.

(6) Assumptions
---------------
- NumA7-1: libjank's bit functions (math.cpp 492-583) shift by a run-time register count, and the shift and bit-test
  instructions take it modulo 64 (x86-64, AArch64). Checked standalone at -O0/-O2 on arm64 and x86-64 (Rosetta); a build
  with UBSan's shift check or LTO constant folding would differ.
- NumA7-2: a native long or double literal meeting a BigDecimal object computes what its boxed form does (math.hpp's
  primitive templates apply the same boost mixed operator to `l` and `typed_r->data`).
- NumA7-3: the S2 fix stores an exact quotient through its decimal text (rd_string keeps ≤ 128 digits by truncation).
- NumA7-4: java.math.BigDecimal on the coordinator's JDK behaves as on JDK 23 (its semantics are specified; the model was
  checked against JDK 23 on every walk question).
