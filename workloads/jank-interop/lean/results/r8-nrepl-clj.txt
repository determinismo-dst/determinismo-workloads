   - stdin op (middleware/session.clj add-stdin 386-397): (.addInput qpr stdin) on the session's QueuePollingReader, then
     (t/respond-to msg :status :done); misc.clj response-for 53-78 turns a non-collection status into #{status} and copies id and session.
     With a cloned session: {:id "s" :session <s> :status #{"done"}}. Without a session the session middleware answers first (round 5:
     session-ephemeral for interrupt).
   - describe (middleware.clj wrap-describe 52-62) lists the :handles of every middleware descriptor; server.clj default-middleware 114-125
     includes #'nrepl.middleware.session/add-stdin, whose descriptor (session.clj 399-406) handles "stdin": :ops contains :stdin.
   - an eval reading *in* with nothing queued (session.clj session-in 44-53): the QueuePollingReader sends {:status #{"need-input"}} on the
     request's transport and waits; a later stdin op feeds it.
   - interrupt and a diverging eval: round 5's JVM measurement (results/r5-nrepl-interrupt-jvm.txt): the interrupt is answered #{done} and the
     eval #{interrupted done}, from the same or another connection; describe is answered while an eval runs (server.clj handles each message
